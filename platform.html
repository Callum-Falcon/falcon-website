The platform

The engine behind every report

Reports are what people see. What makes them trustworthy is the platform beneath: gathering, checking, joining and protecting all your school's data, automatically.

  • Raw data to finished report, handled for you
  • One trusted source for every report and AI tool
  • No more spreadsheets rebuilt by hand

How it works

From scattered systems to one trusted source

Step by step, your data becomes more useful and trustworthy, ready for anything.

1

Collect

Gather up everything

We connect securely to your MIS and other systems and bring everything across automatically: pupils, staff, attendance, grades, rewards and assessments. Nothing is lost, and everything can be traced to its source.

What it means for you

  • No more manual exports
  • A full history to look back on
  • New systems added easily
2

Clean

Check every record

Raw data is messy. Every record is checked: right fields, real dates, no duplicates. Problems are set aside for review, not passed into reports.

What it means for you

  • Fewer “that looks wrong” moments
  • Problems caught at the source
  • Consistent names and formats
3

Connect

Join it all up

Data is organised around how your school works: pupils, staff, houses, forms, subjects and terms, with every mark and grade linked. Measures are defined once, so everyone gets the same answer.

What it means for you

  • One version of the truth
  • Answers across systems
  • History kept year on year
4

Secure

Protect it, pupil by pupil

Access is built into the data. Everyone sees exactly the pupils they’re allowed to, in any report, export or AI tool.

What it means for you

  • Parents see only their own children
  • Staff access follows subjects, houses, forms or other groupings
  • One set of rules for every tool
5

Serve

Put it to work

Use the finished data however you need: dashboards, printable reports, exports, your own analysis, and AI you can trust.

What it means for you

  • Reports that refresh themselves
  • Data your team can use
  • Ready for AI
