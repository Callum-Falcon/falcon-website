Data and reporting for UK independent schools

A school-wide data platform connecting all of your systems together into clear, secure reports.

The challenge

  • The different systems used by your school contain a wealth of information, but these systems don't communicate with each other very well or at all.
  • Reporting relies on a small number of people, often academic staff who may be quite busy.
  • MIS data is often manually exported into spreadsheets, then combined with outputs from your other systems, which is slow, repetitive and risks human error.
  • Data from other systems sits on its own, so you never see the full picture.

What your school gets

Connected systems

Data from your MIS and all other systems is brought into the Falcon Reports workspace - a secure cloud platform where data is curated and kept up to date automatically.

Bespoke reports

We design & build reports that are tailored specifically to your school's individual requirements. Once these reports are built, they refresh themselves on a schedule which eliminates manual work and saves staff time.

Secure by design

Falcon Reports is highly secure, with real-time data security ensuring that users only see the pupils and information they have permission to see.

Room to grow

Add new reports and bring in data from other school systems as your needs develop.

The difference it makes

Every school is different. Here are some examples of the reporting improvements schools can expect.

Attendance

Spot patterns and emerging concerns early, across the whole school or a single form.

Academic progress

See reports and assessment results side by side, and track progress over time.

Behaviour and wellbeing

Understand trends in rewards and sanctions by year group, house or form.

Benchmarking

Put standardised scores in context and see how pupils are progressing against their starting point.
