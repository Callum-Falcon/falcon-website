Data and reporting for international schools

The same school-wide data platform, built around the way international schools work.

The challenge

  • Your MIS, admissions, assessment and other systems were often chosen at different times, and don't talk to each other.
  • Different curricula and qualification frameworks run side by side, making whole-school reporting hard to put together.
  • Pupils join and leave throughout the year, so reports built by hand are out of date almost as soon as they're finished.
  • Boards and owners need consistent, reliable reporting, often from a distance.

What your school gets

Connected systems

Data from your MIS and all other systems is brought into the Falcon Reports workspace - a secure cloud platform where data is curated and kept up to date automatically.

Bespoke reports

We design & build reports tailored to your school's curriculum, reporting cycles and individual requirements. Once built, they refresh themselves on a schedule, which eliminates manual work and saves staff time.

Secure by design

Falcon Reports is highly secure, with real-time data security ensuring that users only see the pupils and information they have permission to see.

Room to grow

Add new reports and bring in data from other school systems as your needs develop.
